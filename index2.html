01 Who we are

A workshop that behaves like a showroom.

Oryx started as a small accessories counter and grew into a full retail and fitting operation. Today we stock, sell and install — so the part you buy is the part we stand behind.

Every bay is run by a specialist: the auto-electrician doesn't stitch seats, and the trimmer doesn't chase wiring faults. That's how the finish stays sharp and the timelines stay honest.

  • Genuine parts with written warranty
  • Fixed, itemised pricing before we start
  • Same-day completion on most jobs

03 How it works

Four steps.
No surprises.

01 / 04

  1. 01

    Tell us what you need

    Message us on WhatsApp, call the branch or walk in. Send a photo of the car and we'll tell you what's possible before you drive over.

  2. 02

    Free inspection & quote

    We check the vehicle, confirm parts and give you an itemised price and a realistic time. The number you approve is the number you pay.

  3. 03

    Fitted by specialists

    Your job goes to the right bay — electrical, trim or tint. Genuine parts, proper looms, clean cuts. No shortcuts hidden behind panels.

  4. 04

    Checked, cleaned, warranted

    Every job is road-tested and the interior is wiped down before handover. You leave with a warranty card and a direct number to call.
