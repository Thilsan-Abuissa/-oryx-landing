III The process

Four steps,
and no surprises.

The price you approve is the price you pay. Every time.

  1. 01

    Tell us what you need

    WhatsApp, call the branch or walk in. Send a photo of the car and we'll tell you what's possible before you drive over.

  2. 02

    Free inspection & quote

    We check the vehicle, confirm parts, and give you an itemised price with a realistic time.

  3. 03

    Fitted by specialists

    Straight to the right bay. Genuine parts, proper looms, clean cuts — nothing hidden behind a panel.

  4. 04

    Checked, cleaned, warranted

    Road-tested and wiped down before handover, with a warranty card and a direct number to call.
