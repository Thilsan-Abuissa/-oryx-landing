04 How it works

Four steps.No surprises.

The number you approve is the number you pay.

01/04

Call us, send an email or walk in. Send a photo of the car and we'll tell you what's possible before you drive over.

We check the vehicle, confirm parts and give you an itemised price and a realistic time — before any work starts.

Your job goes to the right bay — electrical, trim or tint. Genuine parts, proper looms, clean cuts. No shortcuts hidden behind panels.

Every job is road-tested and the interior wiped down before handover. You leave with a warranty card and a direct number to call.
