A workshop thatbehaves like ashowroom.

i

Oryx began as a small accessories counter and grew into a full retail and fitting operation. We stock, sell and install — so the part you buy is the part we stand behind.

ii

Every bay is run by a specialist. The auto-electrician doesn't stitch seats and the trimmer doesn't chase wiring faults. That's how the finish stays sharp and the timelines stay honest.

Genuine parts, written warranty, itemised pricing agreed before we start, and same-day completion on most jobs.

04 How it works

Four steps.No surprises.

The number you approve is the number you pay.

Message us on WhatsApp, call the branch or walk in. Send a photo of the car and we'll tell you what's possible before you drive over.

We check the vehicle, confirm parts and give you an itemised price and a realistic time — before any work starts.

Your job goes to the right bay — electrical, trim or tint. Genuine parts, proper looms, clean cuts. No shortcuts hidden behind panels.

Every job is road-tested and the interior wiped down before handover. You leave with a warranty card and a direct number to call.
